Crew reporting in the web app: which flow feels smoothest?

Same three ideas at laptop width, inside the Notion layout. A crew member's sidebar shows their own site, alerts and past reports. The page adapts to a phone browser too, so there's no separate app. Location fills in automatically, with no latitude/longitude typing.

A · Map first, report drawer slides in

The crew's page is their site map with live hazards. "Report hazard" slides a drawer in from the right. Pick a hazard, the pin drops at your location (click the map to move it), pick severity, optionally add a note or photo, send.
